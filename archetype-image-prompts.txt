=== ARCHETYPE ILLUSTRATION PROMPTS ===
Design Language: Hand-drawn travel-journal illustration, field notes, vintage cartography.
Palette: Deep indigo-denim (#212f3f), wash tones (#2c4155, #35506a), bone-colored linework (#e8e2d1), cool blue-grey (#93a2ae), warm gold accent (#d98f3f), copper rust (#b5651d).

01. The Cozy Window Gazer
Prompt: Hand-drawn travel-journal illustration in the style of field notes and vintage cartography. Muted nocturnal palette: deep indigo-denim background (#212f3f) with lifted wash tones (#2c4155, #35506a), bone-colored linework (#e8e2d1), and cool blue-grey secondary tones (#93a2ae). Use one warm gold accent (#d98f3f) for focal points and copper rust (#b5651d) for small details like rivets, stamp borders, and underlines. Subject: A steaming ceramic mug resting on a rustic wooden windowsill, with a faint warm gold glow radiating from the mug against a backdrop of distant indigo mountain silhouettes framed by a window sash. Cartographic accents: subtle topographic contour lines across the background wash, a small compass rose in bone linework, and a copper rust dashed postage-stamp border. Loose, slightly imperfect ink-and-pencil strokes with visible sketch lines left in, light watercolor washes that don't fully fill the outlines, faint diagonal hatching, and paper grain. Generous negative space, single clear subject, flat editorial perspective. Mood: quiet, lived-in, cozy. Warm gold light against cold blue dusk. No text, lettering, logos, or human faces.
