About me (and this journal)

Hey there, Zarar here. By day I'm a software engineer, which probably explains the spreadsheets. Every trip on here started as a plan built six months to a year out — the list of places I actually want to see is enormous, so I try to be realistic about which ones I can get to and when.

Then I show up and most of that planning stops mattering. I overpack without fail, I apparently talk in my sleep, and I'll eat almost anything as long as it's halal. I also have a documented history of tripping over perfectly flat ground. Somehow the treks still get finished — someone once told me it's "through sheer willpower and not necessarily fitness," which feels about right.

Broke Compass is where all of it gets written up afterward. No sponsored stays, no ten-best-things listicles, no fixed direction. Just what actually happened, and what it cost me.

Thanks for reading — hope it's at least half as fun to read as it was chaotic to live.
