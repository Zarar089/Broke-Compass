Pursuit of the Foothills of Annapurna

Months of planning, thousands of stone steps, and one long walk up the Modi Khola valley toward Annapurna Base Camp.

22 min read April 2026

The trail, in parts

  1. I

    Part I

    Sentinels at the Window

    Kathmandu · Thamel

    Months of counting days end on the flight to Kathmandu, face pressed to the aeroplane window as the Himalayas rise above the clouds like ancient sentinels, faint silhouettes at first, then something far more imposing, with Kangchenjunga the first to show itself. Then a few hours lost in Thamel’s brick, wood and prayer flags, and a short nap that comes less from tiredness than from what is waiting ahead.

  2. II

    Part II

    The Mountains Ask for Their Due

    Pokhara · Nayapul · Jhinu Danda · Chhomrong

    A night on the road, a bridge swaying over a roaring river, and then the mountains ask for their first payment, in stone steps, thousands of them. Sunshine gives way to rain, rain to falling snow, and the last stretch to Chhomrong is paid for with everything we have left.

    5 min read Read part II
  3. III

    Part III

    Storm at Chhomrong, Sunrise on the Fishtail

    Chhomrong · Sinuwa · Bamboo · Dovan

    Chhomrong makes us earn it: hail first, warmth among strangers by night, and by dawn Machapuchare framed in our window. From there the day moves in waves through bamboo and rhododendron, every stream below promising another climb, with stray dogs for guides, a wind worth opening your arms to, and rain waiting for us again at Dovan.

    7 min read Read part III
  4. IV

    Part IV

    Where the Snow Was Waiting

    Dovan · Himalaya · Deurali

    Coffee on a rainy evening, and forty years of a family’s lodge told across the counter. Then prayer flags lead to a temple where bells, river and waterfall keep time together, a familiar flag flies at 2,900 metres, and at Deurali I touch snow for the first time, laughing, not yet knowing the hardest stretch still lies ahead.
